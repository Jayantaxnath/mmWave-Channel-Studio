About this simulator

What it is: a self-contained, browser-only implementation of a statistical spatial channel model (SSCM) in the spirit of NYUSIM and 3GPP-style modeling: Close-In / Alpha-Beta-Gamma path loss, Poisson-distributed time clusters and subpaths with exponential power decay, and subpaths grouped into angular "spatial lobes" for azimuth AoA/AoD.

What it isn't: the parameter values (path loss exponents, cluster/lobe statistics, angular spreads) are illustrative defaults chosen to be physically plausible, not NYU WIRELESS's calibrated measurement tables. Treat outputs as educational/prototyping approximations, not a substitute for NYUSIM or real measurement campaigns.
